# Challenge variety revision — research, design and implementation

User requested research followed directly by implementation and publishing. Baseline 1806260. The prior challenge pack's levels8–20 contain15–22 arrows, with mean path length3.5–4.0 cells: too little perceptual and pacing variety.

Research: [King GDC difficulty drivers](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1026879/Blockers-Analyzing-Difficulty-Drivers-in) and its public slides distinguish layout (open/narrow, shape, empty positions) and experience styles (explosive, journey, puzzly). [Popcore Tap Away official description](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tap-away-3d/id1568058543) describes larger shapes and changing block forms. [King official blockers guide](https://candycrush.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000754717-Which-Blockers-can-I-find-in-the-game) explains keys opening sugar chests. These support variety and staged unlocking as design directions; no competitor layouts or claimed success-rate thresholds are copied. Our key-arrow mechanism is an adaptation, not a claim about Lessmore's rules.

**Goal:** Thirty recognizably different original arrow puzzles with short, long-path, shaped, large-board and key-unlock experiences, preserving previous progress and mobile usability.

**Architecture:** Offline static content generation, optional Arrow.key/lock strings and pure engine unlock predicates. Every key dependency must remain acyclic with geometric blocking. New challenge save key v2; retain original v1 untouched, archive bests and carry unlocked range but reset incompatible partial moves. Classic stays unchanged.
